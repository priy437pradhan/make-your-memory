What Is 3D Hand Casting?

Hand casting turns life's special moments into detailed, lifelike sculptures you can hold, frame and pass on. The process is safe, quick and gentle on the skin, for newborns and grandparents alike.

Every piece is made by hand and can be personalised with names, dates and the finish of your choice. From baby showers to anniversaries, it's a keepsake that lasts a lifetime.

  • Skin-safe, body-friendly moulding material
  • Captures every fingerprint, line and crease
  • Custom finishes, frames and engraving
